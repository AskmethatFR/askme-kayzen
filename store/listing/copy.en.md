## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
Kayzen holds five habits at most. Not six. The brain does not ask for more, and a short list is a list you still open a month from now.

Every habit starts on a soft goal: five minutes a day. It is a target, not a contract. Doing less is already something. Doing more is welcome.

YOU SET THE PACE
Grow by a minute, lighten by a minute — whenever you decide, in both directions, always available. Kayzen suggests nothing and detects nothing: no algorithm decides on your behalf that you are "ready".

NO PRESSURE, ANYWHERE
No streak to protect. No red. No guilt-tripping notification. A day without practice stays a neutral mark in your history — never a gap, never a warning. Empty days are not counted against you.

RESTING IS ALLOWED
Pause a habit with one tap, take it back with another. It keeps its seat and everything it has already lived. Its screen becomes a resting screen: nothing to practise, nothing to adjust, only the road already walked and one way back. A habit at rest is not a habit missed.

THE ONE-MINUTE RITUAL
One screen, a circle breathing slowly, the time going down. Nothing else to watch, nothing else to decide.

ANCHOR IT, AND FREE THE SEAT
When a habit has become natural, you anchor it yourself — it is a gesture, never a suggestion from the app. It leaves the day's list, frees one of the five seats, and keeps its whole history.

SEE WITHOUT BEING JUDGED
A habit's detail draws its last seven days: a full bar on the days you practised, the same bar faint on the others. The week recap says what happened, with no percentage and no ranking.

EVERYTHING STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
No account. No sign-up. No advertising. No usage analytics. No crash reporting. No network connection: Kayzen runs entirely offline. Your habits are written to the app's private storage and never leave it — we have no access to them ourselves.

WORTH KNOWING
Because nothing is sent anywhere, nothing is backed up either: uninstalling the app or changing phone erases your habits. That is the deliberate price of an app that collects nothing.

Kayzen is free, with no in-app purchases and no subscription.
```

## Writing rules honoured

- No superlative, no ranking, no "#1" or "best" — Play refuses promotional elements in the
  title and in the feature graphic alike.
- No promise of a result: the product is anti-guilt by construction, so the listing cannot
  promise what the app refuses to measure.
- No medical or therapeutic wording, which keeps the app outside the scope of Play's health
  app policies.
- The "worth knowing" section states the data loss up front. A listing that hides it earns
  one-star reviews support cannot resolve.
